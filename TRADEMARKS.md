# Trademarks and brand assets

The code in this repository is open under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). The EASM brand is not.

## What stays EASM's

The following are trademarks or copyrighted artwork of EASM. All rights reserved. They are published here so that EASM's own products and partners place them correctly, not for reuse:

- the names **EASM**, **EASM One** and the names of EASM products;
- the EASM logo, mark, wordmark and every lockup (`packages/brand/svg`, `packages/brand/png`, `packages/brand/src/logo`, `EasmLogo`, `EasmMark`, `BrandLockup`);
- the EASM mascot character (`packages/brand/src/mascot`, `EasmMascot`), her look and her name;
- the animated EASM app icon (`EasmIcon`) as an identifier of EASM.

You may show them unchanged to refer to EASM (for example, "built with EASM UI" or a link to easm.co). You may not use them as your own logo or product identity, change them, or use them in a way that suggests EASM endorses you. A fork must remove them, or replace them with its own, before it ships.

The icon set (`@easm-ui/icons`, apart from `BrandIcon`), the components, the tokens and the motion engines are code and art under the MIT licence: use them freely in your own products.

## Other companies' marks

`BrandIcon` draws marks of other companies (WhatsApp, Meta, Google, Microsoft, Moodle, Stripe and others) so that products can name the services they connect to. Those marks belong to their owners, and each one's `source` field names where it came from. Use them only to refer to those services, following each owner's brand guidelines.
